Privacy policy of the “Eva” app (beta)

Version of October 16, 2026. This is a translation; the Russian version is the official one.

“Eva” is a voice assistant for Windows computers. Here we explain in plain words what data Eva uses, what stays on your computer, what is sent over the internet and to whom, and how to delete it all.

⚠️ This is a beta version. Before sales and release, this document will be extended (data controller, contacts, terms of sale).

1. In short

  • Eva does not sell your data. The app has no ads, trackers or analytics (the website has only a visit counter, see below).
  • Eva’s memory, conversation history, settings and keys are stored on your computer, in the app folder.
  • To understand speech and answer, Eva uses online services (speech recognition, a neural network). Below — which ones and what is sent there.
  • A problem report is sent to the developer only when you ask for it: “Eva, tell the developers…”.

3. What is sent over the internet, and where

Microphone and speech recognition

  • While Eva is on, she listens to the microphone to hear her name.
  • Speech recognition is currently done by Google (a free recognition service). ⚠️ In the beta, all phrases the microphone picks up near the computer are sent to Google, not only those addressed to Eva: this is how she understands whether she was called. Eva does not act on phrases not meant for her, but writes a short text to the log on your computer.
  • In future versions the name “Eva” will be recognized on the computer, without internet (“Hearing”). Then only phrases said to Eva will go to Google.
  • While Eva is speaking herself, microphone recordings are discarded.

Neural network (Eva’s answers)

  • The conversation text is sent to the Ollama Cloud neural network, using your key. Along with it go: date and time, your city (if set) and facts from Eva’s memory needed for the answer.
  • If you have connected GigaChat (Sber), it may answer instead of Ollama.

Screen and camera — only when you ask

  • “Eva, what’s on the screen?” / “what do you see?” — one screenshot or camera frame is sent to Ollama Cloud to be described.
  • Eva does not capture the screen or camera unless you ask.

Backup voice

  • If Eva’s own voice is unavailable, the text of her answer is voiced by a Microsoft service: the answer text is sent there.

4. Problem reports

  • Sent only on your command.
  • A report includes:
  • your description of the problem;
  • Eva and Windows versions;
  • settings (without the city);
  • recent errors;
  • the last lines of the Eva, avatar and panel logs.
  • Before sending, Eva removes:
  • email addresses, phone numbers, keys and passwords;
  • the Windows user name;
  • phrases not addressed to Eva.
  • Only the developer receives the report (via the support bot or the evaassistant.ru website) and uses it to fix the error. If there is no connection, the report waits in the reports folder and is sent later.

5. Keys and passwords

Service keys (Ollama, Telegram, Steam) are stored in the .env file on your computer. Eva does not show them on screen, does not write them to logs and does not send them to the developer. Never send the .env file to anyone, not even the developer.

6. How to delete your data

  • Memory and history: delete eva_memory.json in the app folder (Eva will get to know you again).
  • Logs: delete eva_log.txt and the logs folder.
  • Everything at once: uninstall the app and delete its folder.
  • Data already sent to third-party services (Google, Ollama, Microsoft, Telegram) is kept under their rules and can be deleted only through those services.

Beta tester form on the website

On the “Beta test” page (in Russian) you can sign up as a tester. The form runs on Yandex Forms (data is stored in Russia). We ask for:

  • your Telegram username — to contact you (we do not ask for name, phone or email);
  • computer specs (graphics card, processor, memory, Windows version);
  • whether you have beta testing experience, what interests you in Eva and how much time you can spend.

Why: to choose beta participants and contact them. Only the developer sees the answers; they are not shared with anyone. We keep them until the end of the beta test, then delete them. To delete them earlier or withdraw consent, write to Eva support in Telegram. Participation rules are here (in Russian).

Website visit statistics

To understand how many people visit the website and where they come from (YouTube, Telegram, search), the website uses Yandex Metrica. It collects anonymized data: which pages are opened, how long they are viewed, which site the visitor came from, device and browser type, approximate city, and stores cookies in the browser. Session recording (Webvisor) is turned off. Metrica does not receive your name, phone or email. The data is processed by Yandex LLC under its own rules. You can opt out: block cookies for this website in your browser or use a tracker blocker — the website will work as usual. The Eva app itself does not use this counter.

7. Children

Eva is not intended for children under 14 without adult supervision.
