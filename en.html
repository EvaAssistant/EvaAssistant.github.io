She has a personality

Eva moves, blinks, gets happy and sad. She enters the screen through a portal, and if nobody talks to her for a while, she takes a nap in the corner.

Living avatar

A real 3D girl on your desktop. She follows your cursor with her eyes, waves, walks and reacts to what you say.
